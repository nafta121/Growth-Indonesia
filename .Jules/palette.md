## 2024-05-16 - Accessible Icon Buttons in Gallery Lightbox\n**Learning:** Icon-only interactive elements in components like `outbound-gallery-client.tsx` (X to close, Chevrons for prev/next) were missing ARIA labels, creating a barrier for screen reader users.\n**Action:** Always verify icon-only buttons (`<button><Icon /></button>`) have explicit `aria-label` attributes reflecting their action in the correct language (Indonesian in this case, e.g., "Tutup galeri").
